> 账号运营者 (internet toilet account operator): internet toilet administrator
> 开盒挂人 (open the box and hang people): when someone exposes another person's PII, in an effort to incite attacks towards them
> 被开盒者 (opened): someone who has been the victim of an online internet toilet
> 开盒 (open a box): to dox someone (usually used as a 'service' in advertisements)
> 网络起底 (network digging): to dig up the truth; finding a target's PII and then publishing it online for "online public trial"
> 挂人 (hang people): target a person in the internet toilet/doxxing community
> 投厕 (posting to the toilet): posting content in the doxxing platforms
> 被“投厕” (thrown into the toilet): victims of internet toilet

- malware -
